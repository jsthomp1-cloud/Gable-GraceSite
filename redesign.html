The Raleigh Reality

Your Wish List and Your Budget Don't Have to Compete

In today's Raleigh market, homes with the layout, finishes and location buyers want carry a premium, and many families find that what they want simply isn't listed in their price range.

There is another path. Buy a home with good bones in the neighborhood you love, at a price that leaves room in your budget, then redesign it around the way you live. You pay for the improvements you actually want instead of someone else's choices.

We help you see a home's potential before you buy and plan the redesign that turns it into yours, with healthier materials built into the plan from the start.

Optimize an Older Home

Make the Home You Buy Healthier

Not every home needs a new layout. Sometimes the best investment is what you can't see. Older homes often have aging plumbing, poor ventilation and finishes that off-gas for years. We plan healthier-home upgrades that work with the house you have, on their own or as part of a full redesign.

What We Look For

Signs a Home Is Worth Redesigning

Dated finishes and awkward rooms are easy to fix. The things that matter are harder to change. These are a few of the qualities we look for when a client sends us a listing.

  • A location and lot you would be happy to stay in for years
  • A sound structure and foundation
  • Walls and rooms that can open up into a better layout
  • Good natural light, or the chance to add it
  • No signs of ongoing water or moisture problems
  • Room to grow, inside the footprint or beyond it
