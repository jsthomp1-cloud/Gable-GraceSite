Why a Consultation

The Healthiest Decisions Happen Before Construction Starts

Most of what makes a home healthy is decided early: what's in the walls, how water reaches the tap, how air moves and how moisture is kept out. Once drywall goes up, those choices are expensive to change.

Whether you're working with an architect, a builder or doing it yourself, we help you understand your options, weigh health against budget and walk into every conversation with your team knowing exactly what to ask for.

  • Water: plumbing materials and whole-home filtration
  • Walls: insulation and natural, low-VOC finishes
  • Air: ventilation and humidity management
  • Moisture: mold-resistant assemblies, foundation to roof
  • Rest: low-EMF lighting, hardwired internet and WiFi kill switches
  • Wellness spaces: saunas, pools and home gyms

Questions

Good to Know

Do I need to be in Raleigh?

No. Consultations are held by video, so we can help with homes anywhere in the world.

Is this the same as hiring Gable & Grace to build?

No. Consultations are advisory only. You keep your own architect, builder and trades; we help you make informed choices and know what to ask of them.

What should I have ready?

For a consultation, just your goals and any questions. For a plan review, send plans, specifications or bids at least five business days before we meet.

Can I start with a consultation and upgrade later?

Yes. Many clients begin with a single consultation and move to a plan review or advisory once their plans take shape.

Consultations provide general, educational guidance on healthy-home materials and design choices. They are not architectural, engineering, legal, home-inspection or contracting services, and Gable & Grace does not perform or supervise construction as part of a consultation. Please review all recommendations with your licensed design and construction professionals and confirm them against local codes.
